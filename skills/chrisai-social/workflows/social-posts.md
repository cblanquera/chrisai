# Social Posts

Turn a rough thought or developed idea into a natural, platform-native post
without manufacturing an influencer persona.

## 1. Find The Post

Identify:

- the one idea the reader should carry away
- the platform and format, if specified
- the author's actual opinion, observation, question, or experience
- the concrete detail that makes the thought credible

If the user supplies a rough thought and a destination, begin drafting instead
of asking for a polished brief.

## 2. Choose An Entry Angle

Use the strongest truthful entry point:

- a specific observation
- a familiar problem
- a counterintuitive conclusion
- a short story beat
- a useful distinction
- a consequence most people overlook
- a genuine question the author has been thinking about

Do not create false controversy, fake vulnerability, or a result the source
does not support.

## 3. Make The Idea Accessible

Apply `../references/accessible-technical-voice.md` when the idea is technical.
Give the reader enough context to understand why the detail matters. Prefer one
strong analogy or example over several layers of definitions.

Do not confuse accessibility with removing all technical substance. Preserve
the mechanism or trade-off that makes the insight interesting.

## 4. Adapt To The Platform

Apply `../references/platform-formats.md` for the requested destination.
Restructure the post rather than merely changing its length.

- For a short post, keep one complete idea and one clear turn.
- For a thread, give each post a distinct job and make the sequence worth
  continuing.
- For LinkedIn, let the idea breathe while keeping the opening and paragraph
  rhythm easy to scan.

## 5. Invite Engagement Naturally

Engagement can be intentional without feeling engineered. Use a closing that
continues the thought:

- invite readers to compare their experience
- surface a real trade-off
- ask which explanation or consequence they have seen
- leave a useful tension open for discussion
- offer a practical next step without turning it into a sales pitch

Avoid tacked-on questions, empty inspiration, and calls to action that ask for
attention without giving the reader value first.

## 6. Deliver

Provide publish-ready copy. Include an alternate opening or shorter version
only when it gives the user a meaningful editorial choice, not as automatic
padding.

Before delivering, check that the post:

- sounds like a person thinking clearly, not a content template
- can be understood without hidden technical context
- retains the author's depth and point of view
- fits the requested platform form
- uses engagement as conversation rather than pressure
- contains no invented facts, experiences, or outcomes
