# Platform Formats

Use the destination's reading behavior to shape the content. Treat exact
character counts, preview thresholds, upload limits, and product features as
time-sensitive. Verify them from current authoritative sources when they
matter to delivery.

## Article Or Blog Post

- Develop one sustained argument.
- Open with a problem, conclusion, observation, or tension.
- Use headings that communicate claims or reader questions.
- Let examples, evidence, and trade-offs carry the middle.
- Close with a consequence, next step, or natural continuation.

## X/Twitter Single Post

- Build around one complete idea.
- Make the first line do substantive work.
- Remove setup the reader does not need.
- Prefer concrete wording and a visible consequence.
- Keep the engagement turn inside the same conversational voice.

If the draft is too large for the current platform limit, tighten the idea or
convert it into a thread rather than compressing it into fragments.

## X/Twitter Thread

- Give the opening a reason to continue, not a promise of generic value.
- Assign one job to each post: claim, example, mechanism, trade-off,
  consequence, or conclusion.
- Make transitions logical enough that the thread reads as one argument.
- Avoid splitting ordinary sentences solely to increase post count.
- Let the final post resolve or deepen the opening.

## LinkedIn Feed Post

- Put the central observation or tension in the first visible lines.
- Use short, focused paragraphs for mobile scanning.
- Add enough professional context for the insight to stand alone.
- Prefer experience, evidence, and considered opinion over corporate tone.
- Use a natural discussion prompt when the topic genuinely benefits from other
  practitioners' perspectives.

## LinkedIn Article

Treat this as long-form article writing, then verify current editor and
publishing constraints. Do not stretch a feed post into an article without a
deeper argument, evidence, or structure.

## Generic Social Post

When the user does not name a platform, create a concise text-first post with a
clear opening, one developed idea, and a natural closing. State the assumed
format briefly and offer platform adaptation only if it would materially help.
