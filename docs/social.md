# ChrisAI Social

ChrisAI Social creates and improves articles, social posts, X/Twitter posts or
threads, LinkedIn posts, and derivatives made from existing content.

Source skill: [`skills/chrisai-social`](../skills/chrisai-social/SKILL.md)

## Purpose

Use the skill when a technical idea or rough thought needs to become writing a
broader audience can understand and engage with. It preserves technical depth,
trade-offs, evidence, and personality while supplying the context, examples,
and structure a non-specialist reader needs.

The skill does not normally own scheduling, publishing, analytics, content
calendars, channel strategy, campaigns, or community management.

## Internal Workflows

- [`article-writing.md`](../skills/chrisai-social/workflows/article-writing.md)
  develops one sustained argument for a blog post, essay, explainer, tutorial,
  or long-form LinkedIn article.
- [`social-posts.md`](../skills/chrisai-social/workflows/social-posts.md) turns a
  rough thought or developed idea into a platform-native post or thread.
- [`content-repurposing.md`](../skills/chrisai-social/workflows/content-repurposing.md)
  extracts standalone insights from existing content and rewrites them for the
  requested destinations.

## Shared References

- [`accessible-technical-voice.md`](../skills/chrisai-social/references/accessible-technical-voice.md)
  explains technical ideas without flattening their meaning or manufacturing
  an influencer persona.
- [`platform-formats.md`](../skills/chrisai-social/references/platform-formats.md)
  adapts structure for articles, X/Twitter posts and threads, and LinkedIn
  posts while treating exact platform limits as time-sensitive.

## Common Requests

- "Turn this rough technical thought into a natural LinkedIn post."
- "Write an article that explains this architecture to non-engineers."
- "Make this less technical without losing the interesting part."
- "Turn this article into an X thread and a LinkedIn post."
- "Make the closing invite discussion without sounding like a sales pitch."
